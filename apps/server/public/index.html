Your Framer careers page, always in sync with your job board.

SyncRoles copies open roles from your hiring tool into the Framer CMS every hour. New jobs appear, closed jobs disappear, and nobody has to open Framer.

Framer plugin launching this week

How it works

  • 1. Connect your job boardPick Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby or Personio and enter your company handle. You see the jobs it found before anything is written.
  • 2. Connect your Framer projectSyncRoles creates a Jobs collection in your CMS with title, department, location, description and apply link.
  • 3. It stays currentEvery hour it adds new roles, updates changed ones and removes closed ones. If your job board is unreachable, nothing changes.

You decide what gets published

Publishing a Framer site publishes everything that is pending in the project, not only the jobs. So SyncRoles never publishes unless you chose it: keep publishing manual, publish only when the jobs are the only pending change, or publish after every sync.
