Is your careers page out of date?

Paste the address of your careers page. We compare it with your job board on Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby or Personio and show which listed roles are already closed and which open roles are missing.

freeno sign-upworks with any website builder

How the check works

  • 01

    We read your page

    We load the careers page you entered and collect every link that points to a single job on your job board.

  • 02

    We read your job board

    We load the public list of open roles from the same board, the one candidates see. No login or API key is needed.

  • 03

    We compare the two

    A linked job that is no longer on the board is closed or unlisted. An open role whose link and title are nowhere on your page is missing.

We don't keep the pages you check; we log only the site name and the counts. If your roles load with a script widget instead of links in the page, the check can't see them.

Keep it right without thinking about it

SyncRoles copies open roles from your job board into your Framer or Webflow CMS every 15 minutes, so closed roles disappear by themselves. Manual sync is free.
