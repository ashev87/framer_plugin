How it works

  • 01

    Connect Webflow

    Sign in with Webflow and choose the site. SyncRoles asks only for access to your sites list and CMS.

  • 02

    Find your jobs

    Pick your hiring tool and enter your job-board name. You see the roles it found before anything is written.

  • 03

    Design the page once

    SyncRoles creates a Jobs collection. Build your careers list and job template from it in the Designer. From then on the content follows your job board.

Built for job boards

  • No hiring-tool login

    SyncRoles reads the public feed of your Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby or Personio board. Applications go straight to your hiring tool.

  • An outage does not empty your page

    If the job board is unreachable, nothing changes. If it suddenly reports zero roles, SyncRoles stops and asks you first.

  • Your own items are safe

    Items you typed into the collection by hand are never touched. SyncRoles only changes the jobs it created.

Questions

Which Webflow plan do I need?

A site plan with CMS. Webflow does not allow CMS collections on sites without it. The site must also have been published once; until then SyncRoles saves the jobs without publishing them.

Can it publish my unfinished design work?

No. SyncRoles publishes single CMS items, never the whole site. We tested this: a page change that was waiting to be published stayed unpublished while new jobs went live.

Which fields do I get in the CMS?

Name, department, team, location, employment type, remote, compensation (when your board shows it), description, job page link, apply link and publish date, plus two fields SyncRoles uses to track each job.

What do you store?

Your Webflow site ID and name, the Webflow access token in encrypted form (on our server only), your job-board address and a log of sync runs. Disconnecting revokes the token at Webflow and deletes it here.

Do you also support Framer?

Yes. SyncRoles started as a Framer plugin, and one Agency licence covers sites on both.
