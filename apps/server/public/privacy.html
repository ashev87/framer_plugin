Privacy

What SyncRoles stores, why, and how to remove it.

Who is responsible

Andriy Shevchenko, SmartProcess Lab, Zähringerstr. 24B, 10707 Berlin, Germany. Contact for any privacy request, including deletion of your data: info@smartprocesslab.de.

What we store when you use SyncRoles

  • Your Framer project URL and Server API key. The key is needed to write jobs into your CMS on a schedule. It is encrypted before it is saved (AES-256-GCM), is never written to logs, and you can delete it at any time, which stops all syncing.
  • Your job board and company handle, for example "Lever, acme". SyncRoles only reads the public job feed of that board.
  • A log of sync runs: time, number of jobs created, updated and removed, and any error.
  • Licence and payment status. Payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store card details.

We do not store the content of your Framer site, and we do not handle applicant data: applications go to your hiring tool through its own apply links.

This website

This site loads a traffic verification script from ship-or-die.com, required by the $100K Hackathon that SyncRoles takes part in. For each page view it sends the page path, the referring site, campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium) and whether the visit came from a paid ad click. It sets no cookies and stores no identifier in your browser. It does not run if your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal.

Service providers

Hosting and database: Railway. Payments: Stripe. Sync target: Framer, through its Server API.
