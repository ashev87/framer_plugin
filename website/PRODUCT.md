# SyncRoles: product context

Draft written 16 Nov 2026 from what is already decided. Lines marked "to confirm" are assumptions.

## What it is
A Framer plugin with a hosted service behind it. It copies open roles from a company's hiring tool (Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby, Personio) into a Jobs collection in the Framer CMS: once per click on the free tier, every hour on the paid tier. Closed roles are removed. The customer chooses whether SyncRoles also publishes the site.

## Who the site is for (to confirm)
1. Designers and agencies who build and maintain Framer sites for clients and do not want to be asked to update a careers page.
2. Marketing or people-team leads at startups whose site is in Framer and whose hiring runs in one of the four tools.

## Where visitors come from and what they know (to confirm)
- From the plugin's listing in the Framer Marketplace and from posts in the Framer Community.
- They know Framer and the CMS well. They know their hiring tool. They have never heard of SyncRoles.
- A first-time visitor needs reasons to trust a new, unknown tool with an API key for their site.

## What the site has to achieve
Main goal: a visitor buys the yearly Auto-sync or Agency licence. Second goal: a visitor who is not ready installs the free plugin.

## Proof that really exists
- It works with four named hiring tools; the feeds it reads are the public ones.
- How publishing is handled: nothing is published unless chosen, and the "only jobs changed" mode refuses when a page has other unpublished edits (verified on a test project for page edits).
- What is stored and how: API key encrypted, deletable from the plugin at any time.
- No customers, reviews, logos or numbers yet. None may be invented.

## What must not appear
- Invented testimonials, customer logos, usage numbers or response-time promises.
- Claims about failure alerts by email (not built).

## Voice
English. Addressed as "you". Plain and specific, written for people who build websites for a living.
