SyncRoles: three colour and font directions

Each direction names the sites it borrows from, what is taken over and what is deliberately left out, with the logo, a headline, buttons and a jobs panel in its colours and fonts. The wording is placeholder; copy comes in phase 3. I could not screenshot the reference sites from this session, so they are named, not shown.

1Paper and ink

References: Cal.com, Plausible

Taken over: Near-monochrome pages with one working colour, big tight grotesque headlines, generous white space, real product screenshots instead of illustrations.

Left out: Their dense feature grids and logo walls (there are no customers to show yet).

Why it could fit: Green means 'live, in sync'. Calm and trustworthy; the logo rows can light up green one by one.

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque + Inter

Contrast: text 16.9:1 · muted text 6.0:1 · button label 5.4:1 · accent on background 5.1:1 (4.5:1 is the minimum for body text)

2Midnight console

References: Linear, Raycast, Resend

Taken over: Dark, precise interface feel; thin borders instead of shadows; a monospace accent for facts such as times and counts; the product shown as a glowing panel.

Left out: Heavy gradients, glow effects and 3D renders, which are now the default look of AI-built sites.

Why it could fit: Looks like the Framer editor itself, so it feels native to people who live in Framer. Riskiest for looking like every other developer tool.

Fonts: Geist + Geist Mono

Contrast: text 16.2:1 · muted text 7.3:1 · button label 7.5:1 · accent on background 7.5:1 (4.5:1 is the minimum for body text)

3Warm editorial

References: Tally, Plausible

Taken over: Cream paper, a serif headline with character, friendly and human; plain-spoken sections that read like a well-set page rather than an app.

Left out: Hand-drawn doodles and playful illustration, which would undercut 'safe to trust with a client site'.

Why it could fit: Stands out most in a marketplace full of dark tech pages. The serif says 'careers page', the people side of the product.

Fonts: Fraunces + IBM Plex Sans

Contrast: text 14.8:1 · muted text 5.6:1 · button label 5.2:1 · accent on background 4.6:1 (4.5:1 is the minimum for body text)
