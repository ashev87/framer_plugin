# Design references

Chosen 17 Nov 2026: direction 2, "Midnight console". The references were proposed by Claude and named, not screenshotted (the session could not reach the sites). Preview: `directions.png`.

## Linear, Raycast, Resend
Taken over:
- Dark, precise interface feel that sits naturally next to the Framer editor.
- Thin 1px borders instead of shadows; cards barely lighter than the page.
- One accent colour, used sparingly for the action and for "live" states.
- A monospace accent for facts: times, counts, job-board addresses.
- The product itself as the main visual: a jobs panel and the plugin's real screens.

Explicitly left out:
- Heavy gradients, glow, blurred colour blobs and 3D renders.
- Logo walls and testimonial carousels (there are no customers yet).
- Animated "typing" or auto-playing demos.

## Risk to manage in phase 4
This is the look most new developer tools share. The page needs something of its own: the logo's three rows as a recurring motif, and the publishing explanation shown as a concrete before/after rather than icons in a grid.
