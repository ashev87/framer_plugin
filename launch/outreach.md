# Outreach messages

Drafts for you to send personally, one company at a time, with the details from `targets.md` filled in. Nothing here has been sent.

Before sending: in Germany, unsolicited advertising emails to businesses generally need prior consent (UWG §7). Contact forms and personal LinkedIn messages that point out a concrete problem are the lower-risk route. Check with your own adviser; this is not legal advice.

## A. Their careers page shows a role that is already closed
Subject: The "{Role}" job on {company website}

I came across your careers page at {careers page URL}. It still lists "{Role}", but that role is no longer open on your {Hiring tool} board.

I built a Framer plugin, SyncRoles, that keeps the jobs on a Framer site in sync with {Hiring tool}, so closed roles disappear by themselves. Manual sync is free if you want to try it: https://www.framer.com/marketplace/plugins/syncroles/

Either way, you may want to take that listing down.

Andriy
